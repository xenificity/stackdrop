StackDrop — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

StackDrop does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

What the app stores

Your high scores and your sound/haptics preferences are saved only on your device, using standard iOS app storage. They never leave your device unless you choose to share a score card.

Game Center

If you sign in to Apple's Game Center, your scores are submitted to Apple's leaderboards under your Game Center profile. This is handled entirely by Apple and governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. Game Center is optional; the game works fully without it.

Sharing

The Share button creates an image of your score on your device and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Nothing is sent anywhere by StackDrop itself.

No ads, no tracking, no accounts

StackDrop contains no advertising, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, and does not require an account.

Children

StackDrop is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
